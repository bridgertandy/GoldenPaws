FAQ

  • The best way to book an appointment is to send me a text at (208) 984-9293, with your pet's weight, what services you would like, and your nearest major cross streets. I'll get back to you as soon as possible to schedule your appointment!
  • I do not offer full service grooming, my specialty is nail care. However, I do offer some additional services like ear cleaning and teeth scrubbing to keep your pet fresh between full grooming appointments.
  • No, due to insurance and safety concerns, I am unable to allow owners inside the trailer. Additionally, I have found that pets are often more comfortable in a one-on-one environment. I promise your furry friend is in good hands!
  • No, as long as we have established a safe and secure way for me to access your pet, you do not need to be home for the appointment.
  • All of my services take place inside my mobile unit. My trailer is equipped with everything I need to provide your pet with a safe and comfortable experience.
  • I generally recommend nail trims every 4-6 weeks, depending your pet's activity level, lifestyle, and nail growth rate.
  • It's completely normal for pets to feel anxious during their appointment! Over the years I have worked with all kinds of personalities and temperaments, and I understand that every pet has their own unique needs. I always do my best to be patient, gentle, and understanding while working at your pet's pace.
  • Thankfully, this has only happened a few times in my career! However, if I feel that continuing the service would be unsafe for your pet or myself, I will end the appointment and only charge the service fee to cover my time and travel expenses.
  • My goal is to get your pet's nails as short as safely possible without cutting the quick (the blood vessel inside the nail). In most cases, I am able to get the nails down to the quick, but it really depends on your pet's nail length, quick placement, and individual needs.
  • While the state of Idaho does not require a license for pet grooming, I am a QC Pet Studies graduate and am certified in pet grooming. I also carry business insurance to protect myself and your pet.
